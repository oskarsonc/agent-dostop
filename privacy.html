Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Agent dostop (“the app”) accesses, uses, stores and shares data from your Google Account. The app is a private tool that runs on your own computer and connects your personal AI assistant to your Google data.

Data the app accesses

With your consent, and only for the permissions you approve, the app can access:

  • Gmail: read messages and settings, create drafts, send email, and organize messages with labels.
  • Google Calendar: read calendars, and create, change or delete events.
  • Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides: read, create and edit files.
  • Google Tasks and Contacts: read and manage tasks and contacts.
  • Your basic profile (name and email address), to know which account is connected.

How the data is used

Data is accessed only when you ask your assistant to do something that needs it, for example “find the invoice from last month”, “reply to Ana that I will come” or “add the dentist appointment to my calendar”. It is used only to carry out your own requests. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the request you made.

Assistants are configured to ask you before they send email, delete anything, or accept or cancel an event.

Storage

  • The app has no server. It runs entirely on your own device.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored on that device in a file that only your user account can read.
  • The app does not keep its own copy of your emails, events or files. Your assistant may keep a local conversation history on your device, which you control.

Sharing

  • The developer of the app does not receive, see or store your Google data.
  • When you ask your assistant a question, the content needed to answer it is sent to the AI model provider that your assistant uses (for example Anthropic), only to produce the answer, and under that provider's terms and privacy policy.
  • Your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

Limited Use

Agent dostop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Security

All communication with Google uses HTTPS. Access is limited to the permissions you approved, and tokens never leave your device except when sent to Google to make a request.

Your choices

  • You can approve only some of the permissions on Google's consent screen.
  • You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account permissions. After that, the app can no longer access any of your data.
  • To remove the stored sign-in from your device, delete the app's credentials file or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact
