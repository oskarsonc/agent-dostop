Private tool

Read-only Google access for personal AI assistants.

Agent dostop lets a personal AI assistant, running on your own computer, read your Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive so it can answer your questions. It cannot send, delete or change anything.

Read-only

Only read-only permissions are requested. Sending email, deleting files or editing events is not possible.

Runs on your device

There is no Agent dostop server. Sign-in tokens stay in your operating system's keyring.

Revoke any time

Remove access with one click in your Google Account permissions.

Requested permissions

ServiceScopeAccess

You choose which of these to allow on Google's consent screen. The assistant only gets what you approve.

How it works

  1. You sign in with your Google Account and approve read-only access.
  2. Your assistant asks Google for the messages, events or files it needs to answer your request.
  3. Nothing is copied to any server run by Agent dostop. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Who it is for

Agent dostop is a private tool for a small group of people who use it with their own assistants. It is not a public service and is not offered for sale. The name means “agent access” in Slovenian.
