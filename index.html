Google access for personal AI assistants.

Agent dostop lets a personal AI assistant, running on your own computer, work with your Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks and Contacts on your behalf: find and summarize, draft and send, organize and plan.

You stay in control

You approve each permission on Google's consent screen. Assistants are set up to ask before sending, deleting or accepting anything.

Runs on your device

There is no Agent dostop server. Sign-in tokens stay in your operating system's keyring.

Revoke any time

Remove access with one click in your Google Account permissions.

You choose which of these to allow on Google's consent screen. The assistant only gets what you approve.

How it works

  1. You sign in with your Google Account and approve the access you want.
  2. When you ask for something, your assistant calls Google directly from your computer to read or change only what that request needs.
  3. Nothing is copied to any server run by Agent dostop. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Who it is for

Agent dostop is a private tool for a small group of people who use it with their own assistants. It is not a public service and is not offered for sale. The name means “agent access” in Slovenian.
